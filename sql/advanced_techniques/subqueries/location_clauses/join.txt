Used to prepare data before joinning with other tables

Show all customer details and find the total orders for each customer


SELECT
 *
FROM Sales.Customers c
LEFT JOIN (
SELECT
CustomerID,
COUNT(*) TotalOrders
FROM Sales.Orders
GROUP BY CustomerID
)o ON c.CustomerID = o.CustomerID

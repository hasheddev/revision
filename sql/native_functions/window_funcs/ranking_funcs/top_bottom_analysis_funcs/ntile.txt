-Data segmentation (data analyst), Load balancing(Data engineer)

Segment data into distict subsets based on certain criteria

Segment orders into 3 categoroes high, medium and low

SELECT
NTILE(3) OVER (ORDER BY Sales DESC) Buckets,
OrderId,
Sales,
CASE NTILE(3) OVER (ORDER BY Sales DESC)
        WHEN 1 THEN 'High'
        WHEN 2 THEN 'Medium'
        WHEN 3 THEN 'Low'
    END AS SalesCategory
FROM Sales.Orders

OR

SELECT *,
CASE
        WHEN Bucket = 1 THEN 'High'
        WHEN Bucket = 2 THEN 'Medium'
        WHEN Bucket = 3 THEN 'Low'
    END AS SalesCategory
FROM (SELECT
NTILE(3) OVER (ORDER BY Sales DESC) Bucket,
OrderId,
Sales
FROM Sales.Orders
)t

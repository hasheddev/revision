MIN returns lowest value in a window MAX does the opposite

MAX(Sales) OCER (PARTITION BY PRODUCT) find highest sales for each product. Both Ignore null

Highest and lowest sales across all orders and for each product

 SELECT
	 OrderID,
	 ProductId,
	 Sales,
	 Max(Sales) OVER () HighestSale,
	 Min(Sales) OVER () LowestSale,
	 Max(Sales) OVER (PARTITION BY ProductID) HighestSaleByProduct,
	 Min(Sales) OVER (PARTITION BY ProductID) LowestSaleByProduct
	FROM Sales.Orders

Datetrunc truncates date to specific parts

DATETRUNC(part, date)
month and day are reset to 01 while hor, minute and seconds are reset to 00
It selects upto specified part and resets the rest to the above values

SELECT CreationTime, DATETRUNC(minute, CreationTime) AS no_seconds,  DATETRUNC(hour, CreationTime) AS no_minutes,  DATETRUNC(day, CreationTime) AS no_time,
DATETRUNC(month, CreationTime) AS no_day, DATETRUNC(year, CreationTime) AS no_month
